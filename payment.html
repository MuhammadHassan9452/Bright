Our Process

7 Steps From Consultation to Arrival

Each step is clearly defined. You'll always know exactly what's happening, when payment is due, and what comes next.

01
Step

Initial Consultation

Book your free, no-obligation consultation with a Bright Path advisor. We discuss your academic background, study goals, preferred universities and programs, budget, and timeline. You receive an honest assessment of your options — no commitment required.

Status: Free of Charge
02
Step

University & Program Selection

Based on your consultation, our team prepares a personalized shortlist of universities, programs, and scholarship opportunities. We present this strategy to you for review and approval. Once confirmed, we proceed to formal engagement.

Payment: Consultancy Retainer (Part 1)
03
Step

Application & Document Preparation

Bright Path prepares your full application package — document translations, certification, personal statements, recommendation letters, scholarship forms, and all required submissions. We submit applications on your behalf to selected universities.

Payment: Application Processing Fee
04
Step

Offer & Consultancy Balance Payment

Once you receive your university admission offer(s), you select your preferred university and confirm your acceptance. At this stage, the remaining balance of our consultancy service fee is paid. You then make arrangements for university tuition deposit (if required).

Payment: Consultancy Balance + University Deposit (if any)
05
Step

Application Processing & JW202

Bright Path coordinates with the university for issuance of your official Admission Letter and JW202 (Visa Application for Study in China) form. These documents are couriered to your home address so you can proceed with visa application.

Payment: Visa Processing Service Fee
06
Step

Admission & University Full Tuition

After your X1 student visa is approved, you pay remaining university tuition and accommodation fees directly to the university through approved channels. Bright Path guides you through each payment method to ensure safe, verifiable transfers.

Payment: Remaining Tuition to University
07
Step

Visa, Arrival Support & Airport Pickup

We book airport pickup and provide pre-departure briefing. On arrival day, our team meets you at the airport, takes you to campus, and helps settle you in. Bright Path's on-going support continues throughout your study journey.

Payment: Arrival & Pickup Service Fee
Payment Methods

Approved & Secure Payment Channels

All payments are processed through official, verifiable channels. We never request cash or untraceable transfers. You will always receive formal receipts.

Alipay

Direct Alipay transfer to verified Bright Path and university merchant accounts. Fast, instant, and fully traceable receipts for every transaction.

International Bank Transfer (SWIFT)

Official SWIFT wire transfer to verified Bright Path company bank accounts and direct-to-university accounts. Full SWIFT copies provided for records and visa applications.

Other Approved Methods

Additional approved payment options include WeChat Pay for China-based payers, UnionPay, and university-designated payment portals. Specifics are shared on a case-by-case basis.
