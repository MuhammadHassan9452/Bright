Policy 01

Refund Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2025

At Bright Path, we are committed to delivering a professional, high-quality service experience. Our refund policy is designed to be fair, transparent, and clearly communicated before any engagement begins. Refund eligibility is determined based on the stage of service delivery at the time of cancellation request.

1. Consultancy Service Fees

  • Before application submission (0% completed): 100% refund of consultancy service fees, minus a non-refundable administration and document preparation fee of USD $150 (if documents have been prepared).
  • After application submission, before admission result: 50% refund of consultancy service fees. At this stage, significant work including university selection, document preparation, editing, and submission has been completed.
  • After admission letter is issued (any university): No refund of consultancy service fees. The service has been substantially delivered and the outcome achieved.
  • Visa refusal (genuine case, no misrepresentation): 30% refund of consultancy service fee, or credit toward future service re-application (100% value of the 30%).

2. Third-Party & University Fees

  • University application fees, tuition deposits, and tuition payments are paid directly to universities and are subject to each university's own refund policy. Bright Path will assist in facilitating refund requests but cannot guarantee university approvals.
  • Embassy visa fees, courier charges, document notarization, credential evaluation, and other third-party administrative fees are non-refundable once paid, regardless of outcome.
  • CSC / Scholarship nomination fees are non-refundable once nomination has been submitted to the relevant authority.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

  • Refunds will not be approved in cases of misrepresentation, falsified documents, failure to disclose material information, or provision of fraudulent academic or personal records.
  • No refund is available if a student voluntarily withdraws or defers after having accepted an issued admission letter.
  • No refund applies once the student has physically arrived in China and utilized Bright Path arrival / airport pickup services.
  • Services already rendered at each milestone (as per the payment process) are not refundable.

4. Refund Process & Timeline

  • All refund requests must be submitted in writing via email to brightpathinternationaledu@gmail.com with complete details, supporting documentation, and the reason for the request.
  • Refund requests are formally reviewed by our operations and finance teams within 10 working days of submission.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 15 to 30 working days from approval date via the same original payment method (where technically possible) or via an approved alternate method.
  • Bank transfer charges, currency conversion fees, and intermediary bank charges are deducted from the refundable amount as applicable.

5. Dispute Resolution

If you believe a refund decision is incorrect, you may submit a written appeal with additional supporting information within 14 days of receiving the decision. The appeal is reviewed by senior management, whose decision is final. Where disputes cannot be resolved internally, both parties agree to resolve the matter through good-faith mediation before pursuing any formal legal remedy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2025

Bright Path Education Consultancy ("Bright Path", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of every student, parent, and partner we work with. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share personal information when you use our services, website, and contact channels.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal identification: Full legal name, gender, date of birth, passport details, nationality, home address, phone number(s), email address, and government-issued ID numbers.
  • Academic records: Transcripts, diplomas, degrees, certificates, standardised test scores (IELTS/TOEFL/HSK), recommendation letters, study plans, research proposals, and other application-related documents.
  • Financial records: Bank statements, sponsorship letters, scholarship award letters, payment transaction references, and fee receipts (where applicable).
  • Service usage data: Records of consultations, case notes, communications, application statuses, university admission outcomes, and visa results.
  • Website & technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referral source, and analytics cookies (under our legitimate interest to improve service quality).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver consultancy, university application, scholarship, visa, and arrival support services you have contracted us to perform.
  • To submit applications and required documentation to Chinese universities, government scholarship bodies (CSC), embassies, and relevant educational authorities.
  • To process payments, issue invoices and receipts, and comply with applicable accounting, tax, and audit obligations.
  • To communicate service updates, application status changes, deadline reminders, and university notifications.
  • To improve our website, service quality, training, and internal quality assurance processes.
  • To comply with legal requirements, court orders, or lawful government requests from competent authorities in jurisdictions where we operate.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

  • Personal data is stored on encrypted, password-protected servers with role-based access controls and regular security audits.
  • Physical documents are stored in locked filing cabinets with restricted access and are returned to students (where appropriate) or securely shredded after a maximum retention period.
  • Employee access to personal data is restricted to the minimum necessary to perform assigned duties, under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • We retain application and service records for a minimum of 6 years from the date of case closure, as required for audit, legal, and reference purposes.
  • Personal data is never sold, rented, or exchanged for marketing purposes to unrelated third parties.

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

  • We share relevant student information only with authorized recipients necessary for the service: partner universities, Chinese Scholarship Council (CSC), visa processing authorities, Chinese embassies and consulates, university accommodation offices, and airport pickup coordinators.
  • All such disclosures are made only for the express purpose of delivering the contracted service, on a need-to-know basis, and under data protection obligations where applicable.
  • We do not share personal information with marketing companies, advertisers, or data brokers of any kind.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistical data may be used for marketing materials or industry reports, but no personally identifiable information is ever included.

5. Your Data Rights

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you at any time.
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your information, subject to overriding legal or contractual retention requirements.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw any previously granted consent-based processing at any time, without affecting processing lawfully carried out prior to withdrawal.
  • Right to data portability: Request machine-readable export of records you originally provided to us.
  • To exercise any of these rights, please write to brightpathinternationaledu@gmail.com. We respond to verified data rights requests within 30 calendar days.

6. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website uses essential cookies for basic functionality (form session handling) and anonymous analytics cookies to understand how pages perform and to improve user experience. No personally identifiable information is stored in cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser at any time; however, some website features may not function fully as a result.

7. Contact for Privacy Matters

If you have any questions, concerns, or complaints relating to privacy or personal data handling, please contact our Data Protection Lead at brightpathinternationaledu@gmail.com. We investigate every concern thoroughly and respond formally within a reasonable timeframe.
