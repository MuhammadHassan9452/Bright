University Overview

Tsinghua University is one of China's most prestigious institutions of higher learning, renowned globally for its excellence in science, engineering, technology, and innovation. Founded in 1911, Tsinghua has nurtured generations of leaders, scientists, and scholars who have made lasting contributions to China and the world.

Located in the Haidian district of northwest Beijing, Tsinghua's scenic campus is situated on the former site of the imperial gardens of the Qing Dynasty. The campus blends traditional Chinese architecture with state-of-the-art research facilities, creating an inspiring environment for learning and discovery. The university is a member of the elite C9 League, often referred to as the "Ivy League of China."

With over 50,000 students from across China and around the world, Tsinghua offers a vibrant, diverse academic community. International students make up a significant portion of the student body, contributing to the university's global perspective and rich cultural exchange.
