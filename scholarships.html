Government Program

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

Most Popular

The Chinese Government Scholarship Program (also known as CSC Scholarship) is the most prestigious and comprehensive funding opportunity for international students. Established by the Ministry of Education of the People's Republic of China, this scholarship provides full financial support to outstanding international students and scholars pursuing degrees or conducting research in Chinese higher education institutions.

What the Scholarship Covers

  • Full tuition fee waiver for the complete duration of your program
  • Free on-campus university accommodation or housing allowance
  • Comprehensive medical insurance coverage throughout your studies
  • Monthly living stipend: Bachelor's ¥2,500, Master's ¥3,000, PhD ¥3,500
  • One-time settlement allowance upon arrival in China
  • One round-trip international airfare (for specific categories)

Eligibility Requirements (Placeholder)

  • Non-Chinese citizen in good health, under 25 for Bachelor's, 35 for Master's, 40 for PhD
  • Outstanding academic record with minimum GPA requirement (varies by program)
  • For programs in Chinese: HSK 4 certificate or above at time of application
  • For English-taught programs: IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL 80+ equivalent
  • Must not be a recipient of other concurrent Chinese government scholarships
  • Specific program and university admission requirements must also be met

Required Documents

  • Scanned copy of valid passport information page
  • Highest academic diploma / degree certificate (notarized)
  • Official academic transcripts with certified translation if not in Chinese/English
  • Study plan / Research proposal (800-1500 words depending on level)
  • Two (2) letters of recommendation from professors or academic advisors
  • Proof of language proficiency (HSK / IELTS / TOEFL score reports)
  • Foreigner Physical Examination Form
  • No criminal record / police clearance certificate
  • Additional documents may be required by specific universities or programs

Belt and Road Scholarship

BRI Countries

The Belt and Road Scholarship Program is a special initiative supporting students from countries participating in the Belt and Road Initiative (BRI). This scholarship promotes educational exchange, cultural understanding, and human resource development across BRI partner nations through high-quality Chinese higher education opportunities.

What the Scholarship Covers

  • Full to partial tuition fee waivers depending on category and merit
  • On-campus accommodation or stipend for off-campus housing
  • Medical insurance coverage during study period
  • Monthly living allowance based on degree level
  • Priority processing for admission at partner universities
  • Special cultural integration and language support programs

Eligible Countries (BRI Member Nations)

  • Students from Pakistan, Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Maldives
  • Central Asian nations: Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan
  • Southeast Asia: Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Myanmar, Cambodia, Laos, Singapore, Brunei
  • Middle East and GCC countries, African BRI partner nations
  • European countries participating in the Belt and Road Initiative
  • Latin American and Caribbean BRI partner countries

How to Apply

  • Confirm your country's participation in the Belt and Road Initiative
  • Select a university and program from the BRI scholarship partner list
  • Prepare application documents with Bright Path verification
  • Bright Path submits application through designated BRI channels
  • Await university and scholarship committee review
  • Accept offer and proceed with visa processing through Bright Path

Program Overview

  • Degrees OfferedBachelor / Master / PhD
  • LanguagesEnglish / Chinese
  • Processing Time4 - 8 weeks
